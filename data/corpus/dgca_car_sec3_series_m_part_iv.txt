CIVIL AVIATION REQUIREMENTS
SECTION 3 – AIR TRANSPORT
SERIES ‘M’ PART IV
ISSUE I, DATED 6TH AUGUST 2010
EFFECTIVE: 15TH FEBRUARY 2023 (REVISION 4)

SUBJECT: FACILITIES TO BE PROVIDED TO PASSENGERS BY AIRLINES DUE TO DENIED BOARDING, CANCELLATION OF FLIGHTS AND DELAYS IN FLIGHTS.

1. INTRODUCTION
1.1 The Government of India has recognized the need to protect the rights of air passengers and ensure appropriate facilities and compensation during instances of denied boarding, flight cancellations, and delays.
1.2 The regulations apply to all scheduled domestic and international air transport services operating to, from, and within India.

2. DEFINITIONS
2.1 "Block Time" means the total time elapsed between the aircraft first moving from the parking position at departure airport until it comes to a complete rest at the allocated parking stand at the arrival airport.
2.2 "Cancellation" means non-operation of a flight which was previously planned and on which at least one place was reserved.
2.3 "Denied Boarding" means a refusal to accommodate passengers on a flight, although they have presented themselves for boarding under the conditions laid down, except where there are reasonable grounds for refusal such as reasons of health, safety, security, or inadequate travel documentation.
2.4 "Delay" means the difference in time between the scheduled time of departure/arrival and actual time of departure/arrival of the flight.
2.5 "Extraordinary Circumstances" means circumstances which could not have been avoided even if all reasonable measures had been taken by the airline, including weather conditions, air traffic control restrictions, security risks, political instability, strikes, and natural disasters.

3. REQUIREMENTS AND PROVISIONS

3.1 GENERAL
3.1.1 Operating airlines shall ensure that passengers are treated with dignity and courtesy at all times.
3.1.2 Airlines shall display their passenger charter prominently on their official website and at airport customer service counters.

3.2 DENIED BOARDING
3.2.1 When an airline anticipates overbooking, it shall first call for volunteers to surrender their reservations in exchange for benefits agreed upon between passenger and airline.
3.2.2 If an involuntary denied boarding occurs despite passenger holding confirmed ticket and reporting on time:
(a) If an alternate flight is arranged within 1 hour of scheduled departure: No monetary compensation, but facilitation provided.
(b) If an alternate flight is arranged within 24 hours of original scheduled departure: An amount equal to 200% of booked one-way basic fare plus airline fuel charge, up to a maximum of INR 10,000.
(c) If an alternate flight is arranged beyond 24 hours or if passenger chooses not to travel: An amount equal to 400% of booked one-way basic fare plus airline fuel charge, up to a maximum of INR 20,000, plus full refund of ticket.

3.3 CANCELLATION OF FLIGHTS
3.3.1 Informing Passengers:
(a) If informed of cancellation at least 2 weeks before scheduled departure: Airline shall offer an alternate flight or full refund of ticket. No compensation payable.
(b) If informed less than 2 weeks and up to 24 hours before departure: Airline shall offer alternate flight or full refund of ticket.
(c) If informed less than 24 hours or after reporting to the airport: Provisions of 3.3.2 shall apply.
3.3.2 Cancellation Compensation and Alternate Flight Provision:
In case of flight cancellation where passenger is informed less than 24 hours before departure or after reporting to airport:
The airline shall either:
(1) Provide an alternate flight acceptable to the passenger, OR
(2) Provide compensation along with full refund of the ticket.
Compensation scale when applicable:
- Clause 3.3.2.a: INR 5,000 or booked one-way basic fare plus airline fuel charge (whichever is less) for flights having a block time up to 1 hour.
- Clause 3.3.2.b: INR 7,500 or booked one-way basic fare plus airline fuel charge (whichever is less) for flights having a block time of more than 1 hour and up to 2 hours.
- Clause 3.3.2.c: INR 10,000 or booked one-way basic fare plus airline fuel charge (whichever is less) for flights having a block time of more than 2 hours.
Condition on alternate flight acceptance:
Where the passenger accepts an alternate flight provided by the airline, the passenger shall NOT be entitled to financial compensation for cancellation under Clause 3.3.2. However, the airline remains strictly obligated to provide free of charge meals, refreshments, and facilities under Clause 3.7 and 3.8 during the waiting period.
3.3.3 Connecting Flights:
Passengers missing connecting flights booked on a single PNR due to cancellation of the first sector shall be provided either an alternate flight to destination or full refund of all unutilized sectors, plus compensation as per 3.3.2 based on final arrival delay.
3.3.4 Exemptions from Cancellation Compensation:
The airline shall NOT be liable to pay compensation if:
(a) The cancellation is caused by extraordinary circumstances beyond airline control (weather, ATC instructions, bird strike, runway closure, political turmoil, or government orders).
(b) The passenger failed to provide correct contact details (valid mobile telephone number or email address) at the time of booking, and the airline made documented attempts to contact them.
(c) The passenger fails to accept an alternate flight departing within 2 hours of original scheduled departure.

3.4 FLIGHT DELAYS
3.4.1 Airlines shall provide free meals and refreshments to passengers in proportion to waiting time:
(a) For flights with block time up to 2.5 hours: Delay exceeding 2 hours.
(b) For flights with block time between 2.5 hours and 5 hours: Delay exceeding 3 hours.
(c) For flights with block time exceeding 5 hours: Delay exceeding 4 hours.
3.4.2 Hotel Accommodation and Ground Transfers:
Airlines shall provide free hotel accommodation and ground transport when:
(a) Flight departure delay is more than 24 hours beyond scheduled time.
(b) A night delay (departure between 20:00 hours and 06:00 hours) exceeds 6 hours.
3.4.3 Delay Exceeding 6 Hours / Refund:
If a flight delay is expected to exceed 6 hours, the airline shall inform passengers at least 6 hours before scheduled departure and offer either an alternate flight or full refund of ticket.

3.5 DOWNGRADING
3.5.1 Involuntary Downgrading:
If a passenger is involuntarily placed in a class lower than that for which ticket was purchased:
(a) Domestic flights: 75% of the ticket price including taxes and fees for the degraded sector.
(b) International flights: 30% to 75% of ticket price depending on flight distance.

3.6 DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND ESCALATION
3.6.1 Grievance Redressal:
Passengers aggrieved by airline non-compliance may file a formal complaint with the airline's appellate authority or escalate through the Ministry of Civil Aviation AirSewa portal (airsewa.gov.in) or DGCA Passenger Grievance Cell.
3.6.2 Burden of Proof:
The burden of proving that passengers were informed of cancellation or that extraordinary circumstances occurred rests solely with the operating airline.

3.7 FACILITIES PROVISION (MEALS AND REFRESHMENTS)
3.7.1 Meals and refreshments must be commensurate with waiting time and provided hygienically at the airport terminal.
3.7.2 Passengers must be kept informed of revised flight timings at minimum 30-minute intervals.

3.8 SPECIAL CARE
3.8.1 Priority assistance shall be extended to unaccompanied minors, elderly passengers, persons with disabilities, and expectant mothers during disruptions.
